❓ How it works

Koch method (pick Koch above): you learn every letter at full speed from day one, starting with only two, K and M. Score 90% or better on a check and the next letter unlocks, one at a time, through all 40 lessons (K M U R E S N A P T L W I . J Z = F O Y , V G 5 / Q 9 2 H 3 8 B ? 4 7 C 1 D 6 0 X). Hearing whole sound patterns at speed beats counting dits, so you never have to unlearn a slow habit. Drag the lesson slider to jump ahead or go back.

Groups of 5: random letters in blocks, the classic drill. Words: common words and ham words. Callsigns: random calls like a pileup. My text: plays whatever you type.

How to drill: tap ▶, write what you hear in the box (skip what you miss, don't stop), then ✅ Check. Green = right, red = wrong, grey = missed. 🐢 🐇 change the speed. VVV and BT at the start is the usual "tuning up" call, turn it off in settings.

Farnsworth (🎛️ settings on the Me tab): letters stay at full speed but with extra space between them. Lower the second speed to give your brain time while keeping the sound of each letter fast.

❓ How it works

Two stations have a full contact in Morse. ▶ to listen, 👀 Transcript to see it with the meaning of each part, 🧠 Quiz to test what you caught (name, QTH = location, RST = signal report, rig, weather).

Rag chew: a relaxed, long chat. Quick 5NN: the short exchange most DX contacts use (5NN means 599, a perfect report). Contest: just call, report and serial number, fast. The Chart tab has the lingo, Q codes and prosigns.

❓ How it works

⚡ Reflex: you hear one character, tap it on the pad. Every 3 right in a row adds 1 WPM, a quick answer (under 1.2 s) scores double, a miss costs a heart and 1 WPM. Three misses ends the round. Before you start, tap any key to hear it.

Koch set uses your Koch lesson (shared with the Copy tab). Get 20 in a row and the next letter unlocks right in the game, or use the slider.

🏁 Call Sprint: 25 callsigns like a contest pileup. Type what you heard and press Go. Right = more points and faster, wrong = slower. 🔁 Again replays it for half the points.

❓ How it works

🔑 Key: a straight key. A short tap is a dit, hold about three times as long for a dah. Leave a gap of about three dits between letters and seven between words, and the wolf decodes it live and shows your speed.

🏓 Paddles: iambic paddles. Hold DIT for a stream of dits, DAH for dahs, both together to alternate (for C, K, Y…). The app sets the timing, you just pick the order.

🎙️ Mic: hold the phone near a radio or speaker and it writes down the Morse it hears. If noise shows up as E and T, lower the sensitivity; if your signal is steady, 🔒 lock the tone.

🎯 New gives you something to send, 🔊 plays it so you know how it should sound.
